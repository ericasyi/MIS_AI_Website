Correct AI Usage

My Reasoning:
Using AI correctly means using it to learn, not to replace your own work. For example, asking AI to explain a difficult concept, to quiz you before an exam, or to find a bug in your code are all examples of appropriate AI use in college. You should always follow your professor's specific AI policy and cite AI when you use it, since you still need to learn the skills the assignments are testing yourself.

AI Critique (ChatGPT):
I agree with your reasoning because it emphasizes that AI should support learning rather than replace independent thinking. Your examples demonstrate how AI can help students strengthen their understanding and problem-solving skills without completing assignments for them. However, I would add that even seemingly appropriate uses, such as debugging code, can become problematic if AI provides the entire solution instead of helping students identify and understand their mistakes. Additionally, citing AI does not automatically make its use acceptable if a professor prohibits it. Ultimately, I think the distinction depends not only on whether students follow academic policies but also on whether they can independently explain and apply what AI helped them accomplish.
