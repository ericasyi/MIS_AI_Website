Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware spread across the world by exploiting a Windows flaw through a leaked hacking tool called EternalBlue, locking victims' files and demanding Bitcoin payments to unlock them. It infected more than 230,000 computers in over 150 countries, disrupting businesses, government agencies, and healthcare providers whose hospital equipment and ambulances were affected, with total damages estimated at up to $4 billion. WannaCry was significant because Microsoft had already released a patch two months earlier, proving that simply keeping software updated could have prevented one of the largest cyberattacks in history.
