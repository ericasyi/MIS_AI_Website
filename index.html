This website explores three connected topics: Management Information Systems (MIS), AI ethics, and cybersecurity. MIS describes how people, technology, and processes work together to collect, organize, and manage information within an organization. Businesses depend on MIS to make informed decisions and maintain a competitive edge. The AI Usage page examines the ethical and unethical ways students can use AI in college. The Security page explains common threats such as malware and hackers, along with practical ways to stay protected. Finally, the About Us page introduces the creator of this site and the purpose behind it.

MIS Topic Overview
MIS Topic Name Description / Purpose
Artificial Intelligence (AI) AI refers to computer systems that learn from data to predict outcomes, make recommendations, and answer questions. Businesses use AI to automate routine work and make faster, data-driven decisions.
Cybersecurity Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data from threats such as malware and phishing. Strong security practices help companies avoid costly breaches and keep customer trust.
Database Management Database management is the process of organizing and storing data so it stays accurate and easy to find. A well-managed database is the foundation that reports, analytics, and AI tools depend on.
